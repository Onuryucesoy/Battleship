        How to play
        • Each side has five ships: Carrier (5 squares), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3) and Destroyer (2).
        • Ships can't overlap or hang off the edge of the 10 × 10 board.
        • Pick a ship, use Rotate (or press R) to switch between horizontal and vertical, and click your board to place it. Click a placed ship to pick it up again. Randomize places the whole fleet for you.
        • Turns alternate: one shot each, even after a hit.
        • A hit shows ✕. When every square of a ship is hit, the whole ship is marked ☠ as sunk.
        • The first side to sink all five enemy ships wins.
        • Keyboard: Tab to a board, arrow keys to move, Enter to place or fire.
